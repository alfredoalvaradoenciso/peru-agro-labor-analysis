# Peru Agro Labor Analysis

This repository contains code, data, and documentation for analyzing labor outcomes in Peru’s agroexporter sector.  
The project focuses on wage dynamics, informality, and the impact of special labor and tax regimes on agricultural workers.

## Goals
- Generate harmonized datasets from annual Peruvian household and labor surveys.
- Compare wages between agro vs. non-agro workers, formal vs. informal employment.
- Track long-term trends in labor conditions within the agroexporter sector.
- Provide reproducible code for researchers, journalists, and policy analysts.

## Contents
- `/code/` — Stata, R, or Python scripts used to clean and process survey data.
- `/data/` — Output datasets generated from the scripts (no raw survey data).
- `/figures/` — Visualizations and tables summarizing key findings.
- `/docs/` — Notes, references, and methodological explanations.

## Key Indicators
- Hourly wages by sector and formality.
- Informality rates over time.
- Wage gaps between agro and non-agro workers.
- Trends before and after major policy changes.

## Future Additions
- Interactive dashboards.
- Policy briefs summarizing findings.
- Additional resources on Peru’s labor and tax regimes.
